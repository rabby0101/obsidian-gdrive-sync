# Google Drive Sync for Obsidian

Near-realtime Obsidian vault sync through your own free Google Drive. Works on desktop and mobile.

- Your notes go straight between your devices and your own Google Drive - no third-party server.
- The plugin can only access files it creates (Google scope drive.file).

Source code: https://github.com/rabby0101/obsidian-gdrive-sync
