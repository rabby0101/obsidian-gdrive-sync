# Privacy Policy - Google Drive Sync for Obsidian

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Google Drive Sync is an open-source Obsidian plugin that syncs your vault with your own Google Drive.

**What data the plugin accesses:** Only files and folders the plugin itself creates in your Google Drive (Google scope drive.file). It cannot see any other files in your Drive.

**Where your data goes:** Your notes travel directly between your device and your own Google Drive. There is no developer server. The developer never receives, stores, or sees your notes, files, or account details.

**Sign-in tokens:** Your Google sign-in token is stored only on your own device, inside the plugin settings. You can sign out at any time in the plugin settings, or revoke access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions

**Sharing:** No data is sold, shared, or used for advertising or analytics.
